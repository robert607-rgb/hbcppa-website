Keep the connection.

Membership is open to past presidents. Serving presidents may also apply.

Who can join?

The association welcomes past presidents of:

  • Bowls Hampshire and its predecessor, Hampshire County Bowling Association.
  • Hampshire Indoor Bowling Association.
  • Clubs affiliated to these bodies and kindred associations.

Serving presidents may also apply. The association welcomes ladies and gentlemen, with outdoor and indoor games during the year.

Apply for membership

Download the membership form, complete your details and follow the return instructions in the document. If you are unsure about eligibility, contact the Membership Secretary.

Subscriptions

The existing membership leaflet advertises £7 per year. Please confirm the current subscription with the Membership Secretary before paying.

The membership year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Under the published rules, renewal subscriptions are due by 31 January.

Membership questions

Can a serving president apply?

Yes. The current association website and its rules both state that serving presidents may apply.

Do you play indoor and outdoor bowls?

Yes. The association’s membership leaflet describes games outdoors and indoors during the year. Contact the match secretaries for current arrangements.

Where can I read the association’s rules?

The published rules are available on our Rules and Regulations page.
