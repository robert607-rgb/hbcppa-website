Privacy & website information.

A public information website for the association.

Using this website

This website has no member login, account registration, online payment or enquiry form. It does not set analytics or advertising cookies. There are no embedded maps, videos or social feeds.

Cloudflare hosts the website and processes requests to deliver pages and protect its service. Technical request information may be handled by the hosting provider. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for more information.

Contacting the association

Telephone and email links open your device’s usual calling or email application. Information you send in an email or membership application goes to the recipient you choose. For questions about the association’s handling of membership information, contact the Honorary Secretary.

Images and documents

The crest was supplied for this website. The bowls still life is an AI-generated illustration and does not show a specific club or its members. The membership application and promotion poster are the documents published by the association in June 2026.

Content accuracy

Association information was checked against the current public website on 1 October 2026. Officers, subscriptions and event details can change; the relevant officer can confirm current arrangements.
